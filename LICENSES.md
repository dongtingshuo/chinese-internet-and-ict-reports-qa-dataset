# Record-level license scope

This v1.1.0 release combines separately licensed record sets. There is no single license that applies to every row.

| Records | License | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| The 506 retained v1.0.0 records | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | Keep attribution, link the license, and indicate changes. The original reports remain outside this license. |
| New `IICR-V11-` records | [CC BY 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/) | Cite the source report and dataset; link the license; indicate the QA paraphrase/adaptation; preserve the required World Bank, ILO, or ADB disclaimer in the record. |

The license for a new row is also recorded in `publication_rights` and `source_refs`. Institution-specific citation details, notices, and third-party-content restrictions are in `sources.json` and `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

The new rows paraphrase report narrative facts. They do not include source excerpts or source media. The CC BY license of a report does not grant rights in third-party charts, photographs, tables, logos, or other credited elements. No such elements are included. ADB's Chinese publications state that the English original is the only official text; see the source metadata and notices.

`LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt` preserves the v1.0 license text for the retained legacy records. The root `LICENSE` is a scope notice for the mixed package, not a claim of a blanket license. For the CC BY 3.0 IGO legal code, use the canonical link above.
