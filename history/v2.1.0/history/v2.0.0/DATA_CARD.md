# Data Card — v2.0.0

## Dataset description

A unified Chinese-language QA candidate corpus about Internet, ICT, education technology, public health information systems, labor, and digital agriculture reports. It contains 2,000 records: all 833 v1.2.0 records, 60 AI-assisted source-reconstructed candidates, and 1,107 rule-generated sentence-cloze candidates. All 2,000 are in the recommended evaluation subset at the dataset owner's direction; recommendation does not change an item's review status.

## Contents and composition

- 61 source documents from 50 report families and 10 consolidated publishing institutions.
- Unified TRAIN/DEV/TEST counts: 1,377/300/323.
- 506 legacy CAICT records (25.3%); 1,107 new cloze records from 32 ITU and WHO Chinese PDFs.
- Historical IDs, questions, answers, required facts, evidence, source references, and split values are preserved against the frozen v1.2.0 snapshot. Its canonical record and split hashes are in `manifest.json`.
- Cloze questions include one short sentence excerpt with a single masked value, term, or clause. The package contains no source PDFs, images, figures, tables, or long passages.

## Data creation and review

The 60 source-reconstructed v2 candidates were drafted and checked in one AI-assisted session using an answer-free review input; this is not independent review. The 1,107 cloze candidates were generated by deterministic sentence masking from locally extracted text. No per-record language-model inference was performed for them. Their question retains the source sentence with one answer slot, so the answer was visible to the generation process. These rows remain `pending_ai_content_verification`; record metadata includes a source sentence hash, PDF page, PDF hash, generation method, and generator script hash.

All 833 historical records remain pending v2 answer-blind reconstruction; some historical source files also await revalidation. The dataset owner directed that every historical and pending item remain recommended. No independent human review or corpus-wide gold-standard claim is made.

## Sources and licensing

New ITU and WHO source PDFs are Chinese editions and carry CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO notices. Source hashes and notice pages are recorded. Each adapted cloze row includes the attribution, adaptation notice, license link, and noncommercial/ShareAlike terms. Third-party attribution screening for these pending cloze rows is not complete. The package does not include source PDFs or media.

The corpus has mixed record-level licensing and no repository-wide license. The dataset owner confirmed authorization to republish the 506 historical CAICT QA records under their retained CC BY 4.0 record license; this does not assert a license for the underlying reports. Exact obligations are in each record and `sources.json`.

## Intended use and limitations

Use as a source-grounded Chinese QA candidate corpus. The 1,107 new items are mostly short sentence-completion tasks, so they do not form a balanced set of complex reasoning tasks. Filter on `review_status`, `task_family`, `task_subtype`, `split`, and `publication_rights` for the intended study. Historical splits were assigned after annotation and are not blind holdouts; prior system exposure has not been audited. No retrieval baseline or model performance result is included.
