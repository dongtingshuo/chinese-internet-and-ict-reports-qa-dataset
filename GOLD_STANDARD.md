# Gold subset definition — v2.2.0

“Gold” is the release label for the 2,355 records in the active v2.2.0 files. It is a scoped, operational quality designation, not an external certification or a promise that every answer is error-free.

## Inclusion gates

An active record must have:

1. A registered source and complete record-level rights and attribution metadata.
2. Source-grounded evidence, required facts, answerability, and physical-page locators that pass the package audit.
3. Human-review provenance and a settled human disposition where a disagreement or follow-up was raised.
4. Blind AI reconstruction credit for the active answer/answerability, with no unresolved semantic, source, or locator follow-up.
5. A split assignment consistent with its report family and connected evidence group.
6. No unresolved “revise one” overlap decision that leaves both items in the released set.

## Corpus-level disposition

The frozen v2.1.0 corpus contains 2,508 records. The dataset owner attests that two reviewers independently reconstructed answers, required facts, and evidence for every row, without seeing the prior answers or annotations and without AI or other-person assistance. The blind AI source audit credited 2,506 baseline rows. IICR-V12-0026 and IICR-V12-0028 did not earn blind AI credit and are not in this release. IICR-V12-0030 is excluded because its question scope permits multiple plausible answers in the source report.

The v2.2 review produced 220 new-ID replacement proposals. Eighty passed all current selection gates and are included; 140 did not pass and are not in the active release. The 80 selected replacements are included with their new IDs and their supersession links. Of the 2,508 baseline records, 2,275 are retained in the active Gold subset. The prior v2.1.0 release remains frozen under history/v2.1.0/.

## Review disclosures

The two reviewers are identified as D and T. A third reviewer, S, independently reconstructed designated cases before comparing them with prior annotations, then completed adjudication and follow-up. Reviewer identity, independence, blindness, and no-assistance disclosures are dataset-owner-attested. Reviewer-signed method declarations are not present in the returned workbooks. The public release does not present these owner attestations as reviewer-signed evidence.

Three historical AI reconstruction attempts had protocol deviations. Those original attempts retain zero credit; each query received a separately locked clean source-first rerun.

## Interpretation

Use the record-level review fields and evidence; do not treat the Gold label as an accuracy score. Historical splits were assigned after annotation and are not blind holdouts. Prior system exposure is unaudited. Rights are mixed by record and source; no repository-wide license is asserted.
