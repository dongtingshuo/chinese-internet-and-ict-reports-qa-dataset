# Source attribution and adaptation notices — v2.0.0

The exact title, publisher, source URL, PDF hash, rights notice location, required attribution, translation notice, adaptation disclaimer, and third-party limitations are recorded in `sources.json` and copied into each row's `publication_rights`/`source_refs`. Preserve those fields when extracting or redistributing records.

The 1,107 cloze candidates use short sentence excerpts from 32 Chinese ITU and WHO PDFs. The sources' CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO notices were checked at the recorded PDF pages. Each adapted record keeps the required attribution, license link, adaptation notice, noncommercial limitation, and ShareAlike obligation. The question excerpts are short; no report PDF, image, table, figure, or long passage is bundled. Third-party attribution screening for these pending candidates is not complete, as reflected in their review and rights status.

The 506 legacy CAICT QA records retain the attribution “来源：中国信息通信研究院” and their prior CC BY 4.0 record license. The dataset owner confirmed authorization to republish these QA records. That record license does not license the underlying CAICT reports; their source-level license is not asserted, and the reports are not included.

Other source descriptions and exact bibliographic attributions remain in `sources.json`. Adapted records do not imply endorsement by the cited institutions. This summary does not replace the row-level notices.
