# Source attribution and adaptation notices — v2.2.0

Every active record retains publication_rights and source references. The exact source title, publisher, URL, PDF hash, rights notice, required attribution, adaptation/translation notice, and third-party-content limits are registered in sources.json. Preserve these fields when extracting or redistributing records.

The active release contains 2,355 concise QA records and evidence metadata. It does not reproduce source PDFs, images, tables, charts, maps, or long passages. The records do not imply endorsement by cited institutions.

The dataset owner attests authorization to republish the source-derived CAICT QA records. That authorization is for the QA records and does not grant a license to underlying CAICT reports. No CAICT report PDF or media is included. For all other records, follow the license and attribution requirements attached to each record and source.
