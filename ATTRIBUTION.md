# Source attribution and adaptation notices — v2.1.0

The exact title, publisher, source URL, PDF hash, rights notice location, required attribution, translation notice, adaptation disclaimer, and third-party limitations are recorded in `sources.json` and in each record's `publication_rights`/`source_refs`. Preserve those fields when extracting or redistributing records.

The 508 v2.1 QA records are concise paraphrases of official Chinese-language sources already registered in the v2.0.0 corpus. Each retains source attribution, applicable license, adaptation and translation notices, and third-party content limitations. No source PDF, image, table, figure, map, or long passage is reproduced in these additions.

The 1,107 v2.0 sentence-cloze records retain short licensed sentence excerpts from Chinese ITU and WHO PDFs, with CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO attribution, adaptation, noncommercial, and ShareAlike obligations. Their third-party attribution screening remains pending.

The 506 legacy CAICT QA records retain the attribution “来源：中国信息通信研究院” and their prior CC BY 4.0 record license. The dataset owner confirmed authorization to republish these QA records. That record license does not license the underlying CAICT reports; their source-level license is not asserted, and the reports are not included.

Adapted records do not imply endorsement by the cited institutions. See the row-level notices and `sources.json` for exact bibliographic attributions.
